Algebraic Geometry · Derived Categories

Xun Lin

林汛

Postdoctoral Fellow in Mathematics at the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology. Incoming Assistant Professor in Mathematics at The Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen from September 2026.

I work in algebraic geometry, with particular interests in derived categories, GLSM models, Hochschild–Serre algebras of smooth proper dg categories, Hodge theory, Hitchin fibrations, and categorical Torelli problems.
