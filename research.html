Research

Research

My research lies in algebraic geometry. I am interested in the geometry of derived categories and GLSM models, Hochschild–Serre algebras of smooth proper dg categories, Hodge theory of algebraic varieties, and Hitchin fibrations.

Current projects concern categorical Torelli problems, categorical Hitchin fibrations, and Torelli problems for hypersurfaces in projective space.

Selected themes Derived categories · Kuznetsov components · Matrix factorizations · Hodge theory · Semiorthogonal decompositions · Categorical Torelli
